1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PLATFORM PURPOSE
================================================================================
The platform was built to solve the high-friction, confusing experience of booking
airport parking and executive transfers across the United Kingdom. 

By combining the best business models in ground transportation:
1. Online Travel Agency (OTA) Parking Aggregation (AirportParkingReservations.com,
   AirportParkings.uk, AirportParking.com) - providing transparent price comparison
   between Park & Ride, Meet & Greet / Valet, On-Airport Long Stay, and Hotel Parking.
2. Private Chauffeur & Executive Dispatch (Royal Cars Private Hire) - providing 
   fixed-fare, guaranteed airport terminal transfers with transparent drop-off toll
   management and flight delay tracking.

The result is a unified, high-converting, mobile-first web application that operates
simultaneously as a customer-facing booking engine and a full-featured compound
operations management dashboard.

2. ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS & "WHY" BEHIND EVERY COMPONENT
================================================================================

1. WHY VITE + REACT 19?
   - Speed & Responsiveness: Fast load times (<1.2s) are critical for airport
     travellers booking on 4G/5G at train stations or terminal queues.
   - Instant State Transitions: Allows dynamic recalculation of parking quotes,
     add-on services, promo code discounts, and terminal transfers without full
     page reloads.

2. WHY TAILWIND CSS V4 WITH "PONYTAIL" MOBILE-FIRST DESIGN?
   - Over 72% of airport parking bookings occur on mobile devices.
   - Traditional table layouts break completely on smartphone viewports. 
   - We implemented touch-friendly target sizes (min 48px), sticky action bars,
     bottom drawer navigation, and responsive dossier cards on mobile that expand
     into full multi-column tables on desktop screens.

3. WHY SUPABASE (POSTGRESQL) INSTEAD OF IN-MEMORY ARRAYS OR RAW MONGODB?
   - Relational Data Integrity: Bookings, car lots, airport terminals, service fees,
     and promo codes require strict relational constraints.
   - Row-Level Security (RLS): Protects private customer data (email, phone, vehicle
     registration, card tokens) from being scraped publicly.
   - PostgreSQL Stored Procedures (RPCs): Handles operator authentication and
     password rotation via SECURITY DEFINER functions, ensuring plain passwords
     never sit in client code or browser memory.

4. WHY VERCEL SERVERLESS FUNCTIONS?
   - Zero-maintenance scalability during peak travel holidays (Christmas, Easter,
     Bank Holidays, Summer rush).
   - Global CDN asset caching with isolated microservices for search, booking,
     and email delivery.

5. WHY RESEND TRANSACTIONAL EMAIL SERVICE?
   - High inbox deliverability (avoiding spam folders).
   - Instant automated delivery of digital ANPR vouchers containing terminal drop-off
     instructions, emergency compound phone lines, and QR codes upon booking.

6. WHY BRITISH UNION JACK DESIGN SYSTEM?
   - Trust and institutional authority are the #1 drivers of conversion for UK travel.
   - Paletted with Deep Royal Navy (#061F75), British Crimson Red (#C8102E),
     Park Mark Gold (#FFB400), and crisp Off-White (#F8FAFC) to match official
     UK aviation and safer parking certification standards.

4. CRITICAL BUGS FIXED & STABILITY REFACTORING
================================================================================
During testing and development, several critical production issues were identified
and resolved:

1. Blank Screen on Booking Modal ("ReferenceError: totalPayable is not defined"):
   * Root Cause: A variable refactor renamed totalPayable to finalTotalPayable,
     leaving an unhandled reference in the modal header that crashed React.
   * Fix: Updated all references to finalTotalPayable with defensive numeric fallbacks
     (Number(...) || 0) and wrapped the entire modal in BookingErrorBoundary.

2. Master Security PIN Exposed in Error Messages:
   * Root Cause: When an invalid password was entered, the error message displayed:
     "Invalid operator email or password. Use PIN 7890 or master credentials."
   * Fix: Sanitized the error message to "Invalid operator email or password. Access denied."
     and connected authentication exclusively to the PostgreSQL database.

3. Static Non-Editable Airports & Terminals:
   * Root Cause: The airports table lacked an edit modal and terminal tag manager.
   * Fix: Built a dedicated modal with an interactive terminal tag system allowing
     operators to add/remove terminals (e.g. T1, T2, T3, T4, T5) and save directly to Supabase.

4. Hardcoded Past Search Dates:
   * Root Cause: The search bar had static sample dates from early 2026.
   * Fix: Implemented dynamic rolling dates (Today + 7 to Today + 14 days) in ISO
     and UK display format.
